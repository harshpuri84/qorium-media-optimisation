Qorium · Act 2 · What I would run

I would fix the decision rules before the run

01

Check plate validity

The assay owner checks blanks, dead-cell gate, missing wells and spread. Repeat an invalid plate.

02

Read the DMEM contrast

E19 minus the interpolated mean of the 40% and 50% steps. Predicted 5.6 pts, SE about 3.5, power about 48%. Inconclusive allowed.

03

Screen slots 3 and 4

Mean no more than 5 points below the same-plate E19, at equal or lower cost. A pass means “not excluded”.

04

Confirm, then decide

Independent preparations and days. One-sided 95% lower bound above −5 points, plus cost and quality.

Qorium · Act 3 · Beyond this plate

The strategy changes with the experimental constraints

Only 10 to 20 experiments

More space-filling picks, simpler kernels with priors, wider intervals.

Six random points left this model confidently wrong: round-1 interval coverage 17%.

Cheap, noisy assays

Screen widely after paired calibration on the same cultures.

Expensive, reliable assays

Use for finalists, E19 controls and a spread of formulations.

Incomplete costs

Use a price-scenario grid. Get the missing quote that changes selection most.

Categorical or constrained

One GP per category or a mixed kernel. Enforce limits in candidate generation.

Parallel batches

Condition on pending picks, enforce spacing, re-run E19 every batch.
